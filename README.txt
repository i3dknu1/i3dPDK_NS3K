README: [i3dPDK_NS3K_v1.1]

1. Overview
This Process Design Kit (PDK) has been developed by Prof. Taigon Song and his team at the I3D VLSI laboratory, Kyungpook National University for educational and academic research purposes. It is a Generic PDK optimized for circuit design training and simulation and is NOT intended for physical semiconductor fabrication.

Version: 1.1.0
Release Date: Sep 17, 2026
Supported Tools (Synopsys): Design Compiler, Fusion Compiler, IC Compiler II, Custom Compiler, PrimeTime, and StarRC
Target Technology: Generic GAAFET

5. Contents
/BE: NDM library for ICC Compiler 2 and Fusion Compiler.
/FE: Liberty file for timing and power simulation.
/GDS: GDSII files of the provided standard cells.
/TECH: Other technology libraries for digital implementation.
